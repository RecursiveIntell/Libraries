# P16 — Lawful subtraction, compaction, and invariant-preserving reduction

## Phase

Advanced runtime geometry

## Purpose

Add certifying reduction operators that remove, compact, summarize, or forget while preserving declared invariants and history budgets.

## Why this pass exists

The subtractive runtime is the dual of recursive inference: completion needs not just accumulation, but receipt-bearing removal that preserves what matters.

## Implementation steps

1. Define subtractive operator classes: dedupe, summarize, compact, retire, quarantine, minimize, support-core extraction.
2. Require each operation to declare preserved invariants: replay, as-of query, claim support, receipt lineage, legal retention, or operator-specific proof.
3. Implement dry-run subtraction that emits removal frontier and invariant checks before mutation.
4. Compaction may replace stored detail only when replay/history budget says what is lost and what is preserved.
5. Add challenge API to reconstruct or contest a compacted artifact within declared budget.

## Acceptance gates

- Subtraction cannot delete support needed by accepted claim unless claim is superseded/quarantined first.
- Compaction emits receipt and history preservation report with before/after digests.
- As-of queries remain correct under declared history budget after compaction.

- Do not compact receipts that are required for audit without explicit retention policy and approval.
