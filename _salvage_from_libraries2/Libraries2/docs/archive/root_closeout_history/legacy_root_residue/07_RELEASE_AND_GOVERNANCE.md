The release claim for this finish line is the 17-crate support lane in `SUPPORT_PROFILE.md`.

## Adjacent artifact-owner crates

Adjacent owner crates are real and landed, but they are outside the narrow build-certified claim of the hardening receipt. Use them for the public demo and benchmark, not as a justification to blur the support claim.

## Forbidden shortcuts

- Do not reopen V10/V14-V20 architecture while demo and benchmark work are still open.
- Do not invent new owner crates or schema families for the demo.
- Do not treat prose-only outputs as proof artifacts.
- Do not edit derived mirrors instead of the canonical root tree.
- Do not claim the full workspace default-members lane is release-certified by the hardening receipt.
