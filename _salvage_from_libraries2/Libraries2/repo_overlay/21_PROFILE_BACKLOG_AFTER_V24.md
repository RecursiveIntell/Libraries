# Profile backlog after v24

These are legitimate follow-on work items, but they should default to **profiles or overlays**, not new base specs.

## Candidate profile lanes
- privacy / retention profile packs
- regulated-deployment control mappings
- organization-specific role and duty catalogs
- locality / tenancy overlays
- sector-specific hazard libraries
- vendor-specific certification adapters
- incident taxonomy and pager-routing profiles

## Rule

If a candidate can be expressed as:
- policy tables,
- control mappings,
- role catalogs,
- deployment envelopes,
- or hazard libraries,

keep it out of the base-spec sequence.
