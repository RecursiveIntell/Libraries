
# Profile implementation sequence

## Phase order

1. freeze terminal-horizon rule and profile vocabulary;
2. add shared profile IDs and schema registry entries;
3. land P1 privacy / retention;
4. land P2 locality / tenancy;
5. land P3 role catalog / approval matrix;
6. land P4 regulated deployment mappings;
7. land P5 hazard libraries and playbooks;
8. land P6 vendor adapters;
9. land P7 incident taxonomy and pager routing;
10. close on conformance, release bar, and documentation truth.

## Why this order

- Privacy and locality constrain what evidence may even move.
- Role and approval profiles constrain who may approve or override movement, release, and continuity actions.
- Regulated deployment and hazard libraries consume those policy and authority surfaces.
- Vendor translation is only meaningful once the canonical internal targets are frozen.
- Incident taxonomy and routing should be derived from the final hazard and approval story, not guessed in advance.
